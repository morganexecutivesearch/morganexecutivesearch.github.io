Our Search Process

A structured sequence, adapted to the confidentiality needs of each mandate.

  1. Mandate briefing

    Clarify the leadership need, context, success criteria and confidentiality requirements.

  2. Market mapping

    Research the relevant leadership landscape and define a targeted list of candidates.

  3. Confidential outreach

    Approach selected leaders directly and discreetly, without public advertising.

  4. Assessment

    Evaluate each candidate against the specific requirements of the mandate.

  5. Client presentation

    Present a considered shortlist with structured, comparable assessments.

Board Search

Morgan Executive Search supports boards, chairs and nomination committees in identifying Independent Directors and other board candidates.

Candidates are assessed against the governance needs, competency requirements and composition goals set out for the mandate, and presented in a clear, structured format that supports committee decision-making.

Board assignments are handled with particular discretion, including where a vacancy is not yet public.
